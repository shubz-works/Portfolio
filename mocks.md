### Campus Connect — HUL SIP 2025 Digital & IT case (`Campus App.pdf`, 2 slides)
- **Concept**: "Campus Connect," a psychology-driven campus wellness app that divides a campus into 6 zone types (Focus, Connect, Recharge, Create, Move, Reflect) and recommends the right physical space based on a student's self-reported energy/mood check-in, real-time occupancy, weather, and class schedule.
- **Problem framing**: four named issues — academic pressure/mismatched study environments, a stigma-driven mental health crisis, underutilized campus spaces, and social disconnection.
- **User personas**: four richly drawn IIM Kozhikode student personas ("Achiever Arjun" — ex-Goldman Sachs analyst struggling with no longer being #1, "Networker Priya" — first-gen MBA with social anxiety, "Burnout Rahul" — health-neglecting working professional, "Perfectionist Kavya" — analytical overthinker), each with pain points, needs, and usage occasions.
- **MVP & build plan**: tech stack (React Native, Node.js, MongoDB, Google Maps API, Firebase, basic ML) and a 6-month roadmap (MVP build → 50-user beta → AI/ML recommendation engine → campus-wide launch → 1,000+ active users target).
- **Success metrics**: engagement targets (1,000+ MAU, 75% recommendation acceptance, 4.5+ app rating, 60% weekly active use) plus projected wellness/academic impact (25% stress reduction, 20% productivity improvement, 35% rise in collaborative bookings).

### SHEIN: Brand Revival — HUL SIP 2025 Digital & IT case (`SHEIN.pdf`, 3 slides)
Styled as a mock SHEIN India website/landing page.
- **Premise**: analyzes SHEIN's 2025 re-entry into India via a licensing deal with Reliance Retail after its 2020 ban (grouped with 59 Chinese apps over data-privacy/national-security concerns amid China-India border tensions), with Reliance fully controlling local operations, data storage, and manufacturing.
- **Comeback strategy**: three pillars — strategic local partnership (Reliance's scale/legitimacy), manufacturing localization (Indian design/manufacturing, no import dependency), and regulatory compliance (licensing model with no direct FDI, sidestepping "Chinese investment" concerns).
- **Marketing plan**: digital-first/influencer marketing (Instagram/YouTube micro-influencer collabs), heavy press/media outreach pushing a "Made in India"/reformed-brand narrative, and integration into Reliance's existing Ajio app for distribution.
- **Headline numbers cited**: $75-80B 2024 global revenue target, 50% US fast-fashion market share claim, 2,000 new items added daily, 5-year India ban duration.
