**The brief.** NextOffice, a corporate-office leasing/design-and-build platform, was invisible in the media conversation — a media audit showed 0% share of voice against Design Tree (50%), 91Squarefeet and Officebanao (25% each).

**The observation.** The gap wasn't visibility, it was framing: journalists and brokers alike saw NextOffice as a property-discovery tool, not a design-and-build company — and brokers viewed the platform as competition rather than a partner to route business through.

**The idea.** A 3-phase build-awareness → inspire-trust → sustain framework built entirely around repositioning: thought leadership and CXO roundtables to plant the design-and-build claim, a named "NextOffice Property & Design Expo 2025" to take the story into Tier-2 cities (Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Kochi, Thiruvananthapuram), and a broker-facing partnership narrative instead of a competitive one.

**Execution.** Full communication architecture — authored articles, trade press targeting (Construction Week, Architecture+Design, Livingetc), a video-led "#DesignedForGrowth" campaign with influencer tie-ins, a 12-city/2,500-respondent "#NextGenWorkspaces" survey, and a sustainability whitepaper co-authored with LEED/IGBC/JLL/CBRE.

**Curator's note.** The real work here wasn't inventing tactics — it was diagnosing that a market-share problem was actually a category-definition problem, and building the entire pitch around fixing that first.
