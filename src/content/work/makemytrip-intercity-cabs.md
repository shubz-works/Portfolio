**The brief.** Could MakeMyTrip build a viable intercity cab vertical, and if so, for whom and around what actual travel pattern?

**The observation.** A 220+ respondent Calicut survey found intercity travel wasn't one behavior — it was 48% small groups vs. 41% large groups, a 54/46 leisure-work split, and distinct day-of-week signatures (Thursday long-distance business, Friday commute, Saturday leisure). Five segments emerged, each with a specific pain point: unreliable wifi for corporate travelers, safety and seat-hogging for women, budget-vs-safety tradeoffs for students, and shrinking commissions for drivers juggling multiple apps.

**The idea.** "InterCity Cabs" — a dedicated app with a women-only "Pink Cabs" option, wifi/infotainment-enabled cars, and a 5-phase driver-onboarding framework (WhatsApp/vernacular KYC, GPS earnings tracking, tiered Verified+/Comfort/Budget operator segments).

**Execution.** Separate trust systems for drivers (fair pricing, zero-tolerance abuse policy, dispute dashboard) and riders (cross-platform SuperCoins, 24/7 AI support), a launch GTM spanning digital ads and offline "RoadFest" city events with VR route previews, and segment-specific films like "Safe, Solo, Sorted" for women travelers.

**Curator's note.** The segmentation work is what made this real — five different pain points meant five different product decisions (Pink Cabs, tiered driver trust, wifi cars), not one feature trying to serve everyone.
