**The brief.** Four problems compound on a busy campus: academic pressure and mismatched study environments, a stigma-driven mental health gap, underused campus spaces, and social disconnection.

**The observation.** Four sharply different IIM Kozhikode student personas — an ex-Goldman analyst no longer used to being anything but #1, a first-gen MBA managing social anxiety, a health-neglecting working professional heading toward burnout, an overthinking perfectionist — all needed different things from the same physical campus at different moments.

**The idea.** "Campus Connect" divides campus into six zone types — Focus, Connect, Recharge, Create, Move, Reflect — and recommends the right one based on a self-reported mood/energy check-in, real-time occupancy, weather, and class schedule.

**Execution.** A React Native/Node.js/MongoDB/Firebase build with a basic ML recommendation layer, scoped as MVP → 50-user beta → AI recommendation engine → campus-wide launch, targeting 1,000+ monthly active users and 75% recommendation acceptance.

**Curator's note.** The four personas did the real work here — designing for "students" in the abstract would have missed that burnout, anxiety, and perfectionism each need a completely different space, not just a quieter one.
