**The brief.** Cetaphil Baby ranked 4th of 6 brands in e-commerce availability (79.8%) and was missing from 40% of the pharmacies audited, despite being part of a trusted parent brand.

**The observation.** A 20-pharmacy, 12-platform, 7-city audit plus interviews with gynaecologists, dermatologists, and new parents traced the real root cause: doctors weren't prescribing it. Against Johnson's and Sebamed's medical credibility, and Tedibar emerging fast on the same flank, Cetaphil Baby was fighting a shelf-space problem that was actually a trust problem.

**The idea.** "Authority Before Advocacy" — build medical credibility first, consumer demand second. Phase one alone runs an 18-month HCP network build (500+ doctors, symposiums, a KOL advisory board, earned PR targeting ₹150M+ media value) before any consumer-facing push.

**Execution.** Phase two repositions the brand as "For Mothers. Therefore, for Babies," extending trusted adult Cetaphil equity, with a mother+baby bundle, a hospital-bag TVC concept, celebrity casting (Kiara Advani x Sidharth Malhotra), and a #MyFirst100Days UGC campaign. Phase three sustains momentum via Cloudnine hospital kiosks and a "Growing Together, Gentle Always" lifecycle campaign as babies grow into toddlers.

**Curator's note.** The instinct to jump straight to a consumer campaign was strong — the discipline here was proving, with the pharmacy and doctor data, that no campaign would work until the medical credibility gap closed first.
