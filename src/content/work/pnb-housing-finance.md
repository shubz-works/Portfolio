**The brief.** PNB Housing Finance needed its FY25 performance reviewed and FY26 strategy set — against a backdrop where sentiment had dipped from 70% to 56% positive and share of voice fell from 44% to 21%, even as competitors' own moments (Bajaj Housing's IPO listing pop, Aadhar Housing's surge) crowded the category.

**The observation.** Rising media spend (AVE nearly doubled to ₹467M) hadn't translated to rising share of voice — the account needed sharper positioning of the CEO as a category authority, not just more coverage volume, plus real regional and Tier-2/3 depth competitors weren't building.

**The idea.** Four FY26 pillars: authority-building thought leadership for the CEO, regional expansion PR into the North East and Tier-2/3 markets, consumer education on home loans and PMAY-U 2.0, and HR/CSR programs built for brand loyalty rather than one-off coverage.

**Execution.** A multilingual North East launch campaign (radio jingles, SHG partnerships, tea-estate financial literacy workshops), a "Living Heritage – Homes of India" cultural documentary series tying regional architecture to housing finance messaging, a 12-city/2,500-respondent youth aspiration survey, a proposed 45-day multi-city "First Step Home Festival," and — running alongside all of it — live crisis management on a SEBI whistleblower story tracked across Mint, WSJ, and Reuters.

**Curator's note.** This is the account that taught me retainer work is really two jobs at once: executing a planned annual narrative, and being ready to manage the story that wasn't on the calendar.
