**The brief.** Britannia's ESG pillars — education, healthcare, waste management — cared visibly for employees, but said nothing about the gig delivery partners (e.g. Zepto riders) who touch the brand every day.

**The observation.** Mapping a delivery partner's actual journey — order assignment, store navigation, pickup, customer delivery — surfaced real anxieties at each stage: safety, penalties, earnings, customer blame. That gap between "cared for" and "extended community, ignored" was the opening.

**The idea.** "Road Safety for Gig Heroes" (Britannia x Zepto): riders earn "Kudos" and join a "Selfie a Smile" campaign; hitting 100+ Kudos or 50+ selfies a month unlocks road safety insurance up to ₹1 lakh via the Sir Ness Wadia Foundation. Customers get 5% off for engaging — a triple win of rider safety, brand goodwill/UGC, and a customer discount.

**Execution.** A multi-channel activation plan spanning merch, regional recognition events, digital badges, press releases, influencer collaborations, and in-app Zepto notifications, plus mocked ad creatives and an app-flow mockup of the Kudos/Selfie mechanic.

**Curator's note.** The strongest ESG ideas aren't the ones that add a new initiative — they're the ones that notice who the existing pillars quietly left out.
