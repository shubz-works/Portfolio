**The brief.** SHEIN was banned in India in 2020 alongside 59 other Chinese apps over data-privacy and national-security concerns amid border tensions. Its 2025 re-entry needed to solve the actual reason it was banned, not just relaunch marketing.

**The observation.** A straight relaunch would re-trigger the same "Chinese investment" objections — the licensing deal with Reliance Retail mattered strategically, not just commercially, because it moved data storage, manufacturing, and operational control fully onshore.

**The idea.** A three-pillar comeback: strategic local partnership (Reliance's scale and legitimacy), manufacturing localization (Indian design and production, no import dependency), and regulatory compliance (a licensing model with no direct FDI).

**Execution.** A digital-first, micro-influencer-led marketing push paired with heavy press outreach carrying a "Made in India," reformed-brand narrative, distributed through Reliance's existing Ajio app rather than a standalone platform.

**Curator's note.** The marketing plan was the easy part — the actual strategic move was recognizing that the ban was a trust and sovereignty issue, so the re-entry structure itself had to be the message.
