**The brief.** A first-ever "Art x Scent" concept for Lancôme: could a fragrance line carry cultural and artistic identity rather than just a mood or occasion?

**The observation.** The target customer — a "Cultured Connoisseur," agender and age-agnostic across Gen Z, Millennials, and HNW collectors — wants originality and ethically-sourced luxury they can point to as a considered choice, not just a signature scent.

**The idea.** Five fragrances, each translating one iconic painting/artist into a scent profile: Yayoi Kusama (Japan), Raja Ravi Varma (India), Frida Kahlo (Mexico), Vermeer (Netherlands), Bisa Butler (Black Diaspora/USA) — housed in a sculptural, infinitely-refillable "L-shaped" glass bottle designed as a keep-forever artifact.

**Execution.** Osmobloom™ eco-extraction (water/heat/solvent-free), 95% bio-based formulas, ethically-sourced Haitian vetiver, in-store refill fountains cutting packaging use 44–100% vs. repurchase, and a "phygital" gallery retail experience — AR masterpiece frames synced to scent diffusion and a home AR extension via the bottle itself.

**Curator's note.** This one started from a genuine question — can a fragrance carry as much meaning as the painting it's named after — and the sustainability and retail mechanics were built to prove the concept could actually ship, not just look good on a slide.
