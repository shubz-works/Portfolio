### Airtel — iCREATE 2026 (`Airtel.pdf`, 5 slides)
Team: WeCreate (Shubhangi Goyal, Jagrati, Vinay Garg), IIM Kozhikode.
- **Problem framing**: Primary research (100+ consumer interviews, word-cloud of pain points around "service"/"support") identifies 5 barriers to telecom bundling adoption — bundling perceived as lock-in not convenience, cross-service hostage effect (one broken service blocks unrelated closures), punitive unbundling/exit process, customer service trust deficit, "frictionless" promise vs reality gap.
- **Secondary research**: cites ET, EY, DoT, JioThings, Redseer stats on India's smart-home market (₹36,000cr by 2028) and telcos shifting from bandwidth providers to AI-driven platform/ecosystem players.
- **Solution — "Altura Home Intelligence"**: an AI-driven HomeOS that learns household members/routines/OTT preferences/security needs and self-manages bandwidth, family modes, subscriptions, and security — pitched as turning Airtel from a bundle-of-services into one self-managing digital home product, with a mobile app mockup shown.
- **Go-to-market**: acquisition (awareness campaigns, real-estate/builder partnerships, experience marketing, seamless activation) vs. expansion/retention (AI household optimizer, cross-selling, retention via health reports).
- **Customer journey** mapped across 7 stages (Discover → Purchase/Move-in → Onboarding → Autonomous management → Predictive self-healing support → Human escalation → Continuous loyalty evolution), each tied to a specific Altura touchpoint and AI role.

### Britannia — "Creatovate" Sales & Marketing (`Britannia.pdf`, 4 slides)
Handle credited: @Jugshu.
- **Theme**: ESG-driven sales & marketing idea centered on last-mile gig delivery workers (e.g. Zepto delivery partners) as an underserved "extended community" touchpoint for Britannia's ESG story.
- **Insight work**: maps the gig worker's delivery journey (order assignment → store navigation → pickup → customer delivery) with the anxieties/pain points at each stage (safety, penalties, earnings, customer blame), then reframes existing Britannia ESG pillars (education, healthcare, waste management, etc.) around a gap: employees are cared for, but extended gig partners are not.
- **Big idea — "Road Safety for Gig Heroes" (Britannia x Zepto)**: two-wheeler delivery partners earn "Kudos" and participate in a "Selfie a Smile" campaign; hitting 100+ Kudos or 50+ selfies/month unlocks road safety insurance up to ₹1 lakh (via Sir Ness Wadia Foundation). Customers get 5% off for engaging: a triple-win loop (safety for riders, brand goodwill/UGC for Britannia, discount for customers). Pitched as India's first FMCG brand insuring gig workers directly.
- **Campaign execution**: multi-channel plan (merch, regional recognition events, digital badges, press releases, influencer collabs, in-app Zepto notifications) plus mocked ad creatives/headlines and a phone-app mockup of the Kudos/Selfie flow.

### Lancôme / L'Oréal — "Maison Lancôme: The Art of Identity" (`Lancome_Loreal.pdf`, 3 slides)
New-product/brand-experience concept for L'Oréal Luxe's Lancôme.
- **Concept**: a first-ever "Art x Scent" fragrance collection translating 5 iconic paintings/artists (Yayoi Kusama–Japan, Raja Ravi Varma–India, Frida Kahlo–Mexico, Vermeer–Netherlands, Bisa Butler–Black Diaspora/USA) into 5 distinct fragrances, each with named scent profile, key notes, and narrative "vibe."
- **Product design**: signature "L-shaped" sculptural, infinitely-refillable glass bottle positioned as a "keep-forever" artifact; Osmobloom™ eco-extraction (water/heat/solvent-free scent capture), 95% bio-based formulas, ethical sourcing (Haitian vetiver), in-store refill fountains claimed to cut glass/plastic/metal use 44–100% vs. repurchase.
- **Target customer**: the "Cultured Connoisseur" — agender/age-agnostic Gen Z–Millennial + HNW collectors seeking originality and ethically-sourced luxury.
- **Retail experience**: "Phygital" gallery concept — AR masterpiece frames synced to scent diffusion, algorithmic scent-identity-mapping kiosks, and a home AR extension via scanning the bottle.
- **Business case**: rollout via flagship stores (Paris/NY/Shanghai/Dubai) then 3,000+ Lancôme doors leveraging existing L'Oréal production/supply chain; SKU pricing ($125/$200/$140), and KPIs (40% refill adoption, 1mn+ #fragrancetok views, 500k kiosk users, 30-market rollout, 70% gross margin).

### Galderma — "GRAD 2.0" Cetaphil Baby Growth Strategy (`Galderma.pdf`, 8 slides)
Team: RealLove 2.0 (Jagrati, Shubhangi Goyal, Pooja Ojha), IIM Kozhikode.
- **Research**: primary interviews with gynaecologists, dermatologists, and new parents on baby-skincare purchase drivers (doctor recommendation, safety, ingredient transparency, co-parenting decisions); secondary research frames the market via digital/omnichannel, premiumization/safety, and medical credibility factors.
- **Competitive diagnosis**: 20-pharmacy + 12-platform/7-city audit shows Cetaphil Baby ranks 4th of 6 brands in e-commerce availability (79.8%) and suffers poor shelf placement (40% of pharmacies don't stock it) — root cause traced to a "doctor doesn't prescribe it" gap versus Johnson's/Sebamed, with Tedibar flagged as the sharpest emerging threat on the medical-credibility flank.
- **3-phase strategy — "Authority Before Advocacy"**:
  1. *Foundation Building* (Nov 2025–Apr 2026): build medical authority via a 500+ HCP network, symposiums, KOL advisory board, and earned PR (targeting ₹150M+ media value) — establishing "gentle infant skin care" association before any product push.
  2. *Reaching the Masses*: repositions Cetaphil Baby as an extension of trusted adult Cetaphil ("For Mothers. Therefore, for Babies"), with a mother+baby bundle, a YouTube commercial storyboard (hospital-bag/first-embrace narrative), celebrity casting ideas (Kiara Advani x Sidharth Malhotra), an influencer/#MyFirst100Days UGC campaign, and omnichannel distribution (HCP endorsements, hospital tie-ups, pharmacy chain partnerships, quick-commerce placement).
  3. *Sustaining the Momentum*: Cloudnine hospital kiosk rollout and a "Growing Together, Gentle Always" lifecycle campaign extending messaging as babies grow into toddlers.
- **Business case**: full 2-year quarterly roadmap, phase-wise budget (~₹6.8–7.1 Cr total across 3 phases), a comprehensive KPI tracker (HCP onboarding, retail/e-comm uplift, brand recall, kiosk activation), and a risk/mitigation matrix (low HCP adoption, competitor incentivization, celeb-less digital reach, reduced sampling, Tier 2/3 price sensitivity).

### MakeMyTrip — "InterCity Cabs" New Vertical (`MakeMyTrip (1).pdf`, 9 slides)
Team: Saumya Srivastava, Shubhangi Goyal, Vignesh Raman, IIM Kozhikode. Tagline: "Helping the World Meet."
- **Market analysis**: 3-day, 220+ respondent on-ground survey in Calicut on intercity travel patterns — group size mix (48% small groups, 41% large groups), average 3.8-hr trips, 54% leisure/46% work split, with distinct Thursday (long-distance business), Friday (regular commute) and Saturday (leisure/random) travel signatures.
- **Segments identified**: leisure, corporate, student, necessity (emergency), and female travelers — each profiled with a specific pain point quote (e.g. corporate: unreliable Wi-Fi/delays; female: safety/seat-hogging; students: budget vs. safety trade-off; full-time drivers: shrinking commissions from multi-apping).
- **Product — InterCity Cabs app**: full mobile mockups covering phone/OTP login, city selection (7 states), ride booking (passengers, source/destination, immediate vs scheduled), women-only "Pink Cabs" option, wifi/infotainment-enabled cars, pre/post-payment choice, and booking confirmation.
- **Supply-side digitization**: a 5-phase driver-onboarding framework (identify/target → simplify onboarding via WhatsApp/vernacular KYC → enable digitally with GPS/earnings tracker → segment operators into Verified+/Comfort/Budget tiers → monitor & incentivize), plus an operator app feature table and incentive structure (on-time bonus, high-rating perks, referral bonus).
- **Trust & transparency plan**: separate initiatives for drivers (fair pricing, zero-tolerance abuse policy, dispute dashboard, regional leaderboards) and customers (SuperCoins cross-platform rewards, 24/7 AI support, personalized recommendations, feedback loops).
- **GTM strategy**: online (targeted digital ads, travel/corporate/regional influencers) and offline (MMT RoadFest multi-city launch events with VR route experiences, regional press conferences, government/tourism-board tie-ups) plus a 90-second launch film and segment-specific video campaigns (e.g. "Safe, Solo, Sorted" for women, "Budget Mein Ghumna" for students).
- **Implementation & targets**: 16-week phased rollout (planning → design → dev → testing → deployment → post-deployment) targeting 50,000 downloads, 2,500 verified drivers, ₹100 Cr revenue run-rate and 15% market share within 6 months.

### Reckitt — "Reckitt Career Compass" Distributor Case (`Reckitt_Case Comp.pdf`, 17 slides)
Team: Shubhangi Goyal, Pooja Ojha, Abinaya, IIM Kozhikode. This is a campus recruitment case competition (case brief + solution combined in one deck), evaluated on problem-statement identification, subject understanding, rationale, and presentation.
- **Case prompt**: as a newly-joined Area Sales Manager (Delhi Metro, ₹220 Cr territory), diagnose why a top distributor (Mr. Rajesh, Scootsy Marketing, ₹3.5 Cr/month billing) is underperforming during festive season — symptoms include excess stock (₹1.8 Cr vs 7-day norm), ₹1 Cr market credit outstanding, a WS margin squeeze request (4.75%→6.25%), wholesale share creeping from 25%→32% while retail declines, VI payout collapsing from 1.5%→0.6%, and rising salesman attrition (take-home ₹4,000→₹2,000). Includes 7 structured data exhibits (P&L/ops, VI parameters, assortment/channel, retail-vs-WS split, outlet expansion, retail→MT/e-comm shift, training) and 6 required analysis questions.
- **Team's solution — problem diagnosis & distributor decision**: builds a weighted decision matrix (11 criteria × 100 points) scoring Continue/Renegotiate/Replace — Renegotiate wins (775 vs 419/669) — with a 4-part "way forward": working capital optimization, tightening the credit cycle, salesforce revitalization, and rebalancing channel mix.
- **Channel & assortment strategy**: diagnoses the wholesale-share creep as sales-force incentive misalignment, proposes resetting WS discounts to 2.5-3%, introducing retail-exclusive SKU packs, strengthening DMS/primary-secondary sales tracking, retailer loyalty schemes, and a full assortment-rationalization plan (SKU count 120→85-90, slow-movers 35%→10-15%).
- **ROI & working capital analysis**: computes distributor ROI (61.6%) via Net Profit/Capital Employed with a stated 15% cost-of-capital assumption, benchmarks holding days/ROI against Indian FMCG averages, and proposes 5 additional data points (regional seasonality, competitor margins, retailer sell-out data, dealer credit ratings) to sharpen the recommendation.
- **Salesforce incentives & training**: redesigns the VI scheme around Expectancy/Equity/Goal-Setting theory (coverage, sales volume, order value, training certification components), a tiered Gold/Silver/Bronze reward system, a training calendar with budgeted hours/cost per module, and a calculated training ROI (1,293%) — plus an AI performance dashboard/leaderboard mockup for salesperson gamification.
- **Implementation**: 12-month quarterly roadmap across 3 initiatives (WC/credit optimization, channel conflict resolution, salesforce revitalization) with named owners (ASM+Finance, ASM+Trade Mktg, ASM+HR), KPIs, risk/mitigation table, a steering committee structure, and a salesperson-journey map from onboarding to promotion.
